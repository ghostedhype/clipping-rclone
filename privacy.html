Privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

clipping-rclone is a personal tool used only by its owner. When the owner signs in with Google, the tool uses Google's official APIs to read and write the owner's own Google Drive files and to upload videos to, and read statistics for, the owner's own YouTube channels.

What data is used

Only the signed-in owner's own Drive files, YouTube videos and channel statistics. Access tokens are stored on the owner's own computer.

What is not done

No data is collected from anyone else. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
